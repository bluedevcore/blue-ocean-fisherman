## Idea (neutral statement)
- Problem: Lenders that fund dealer stock (UK "stocking finance" / US "floorplan") verify that each funded vehicle is still on the dealer's site through periodic physical audits by firms such as SGS, RGIS and DEKRA, or by photo/number-plate "digital audit" apps. Between audits a dealer can sell a funded car and keep the proceeds ("sold out of trust"); when a dealer fails, the lender can lose large sums and may lose title to cars sold to good-faith buyers.
- Who suffers / who pays: Lender credit / dealer-risk teams (loss and audit cost); audit contractors; dealers who host audits.
- Sensor concept: BLE tag (windscreen sticker or key-fob) per funded vehicle, plus an LTE gateway at the dealer site; daily presence report per VIN, alert if a funded car is absent for more than ~24 h without settlement; physical audits reserved for exceptions.

## 3. Current workarounds
- Physical audit: RGIS sent "one auditor per 100 vehicles" to complete a manufacturer finance arm's dealership floor-plan count within a few hours; some sites held over 1,000 vehicles; audits also checked whether a vehicle had been sold and delivered but not paid out (vendor case study, UK site) (search extract) — https://www.rgis.co.uk/case-study/vehicle-dealership-stock-audit-service/ ; https://www.rgis.co.uk/Documents/case-studies/mws/Vehicle-Dealership-Stock-Audit-Service-EN.pdf
- SGS describes its vehicle stock audit as a "vital tool in managing risk in floorplan lending" (vendor, UK page) (search extract) — https://www.sgs.com/en-gb/services/vehicle-stock-audit
- DEKRA sells automotive stock audits giving dealers and their financial partners an accounting and status of floorplan inventory (vendor) (search extract) — https://www.dekra.com/en/automotive-stock-audits/
- Definition: a floorplan audit is the process by which the lender verifies all inventory and documentation provided by the dealer (US) (search extract) — https://www.autofinance.com/insights/floorplan-audit
- Digital audit (number-plate scanning): "VWFS cuts vehicle audit times by 90%" (Fleet News, Sept 2022); a Tees Valley software firm signed Volkswagen Financial Services as a customer — described in results as CheckPlate (Coeus Software), an Android app plus cloud service that scans plates against the stock list (vendor claim, UK) (search extract) — https://www.fleetnews.co.uk/news/fleet-industry-news/2022/09/16/vwfs-cuts-vehicle-audit-times-by-90 ; https://bdaily.co.uk/articles/2022/09/07/tees-valley-company-signs-volkswagen-fs-as-customer
- Dealer self-audit: V12 Vehicle Finance's "SOAR" lets its dealer network "securely self-audit wholesale-funded inventory" alongside traditional audits (2019 press release, UK) (search extract) — https://pressat.co.uk/releases/v12-vehicle-finance-flies-with-soar-to-revolutionise-wholesale-funded-stock-audits-6733d9260a25abed18f9b107dbf69b11

## 9. Recurring revenue
- Audits are already a recurring paid service (monthly to bi-annual cycles per RGIS case studies), so a per-funded-vehicle-per-month fee would replace/reduce a recurring line item rather than create a new one (search extract) — https://www.rgis.co.uk/case-study/agricultural-vehicle-stock-verification-service/ ; https://www.rgis.co.uk/case-study/vehicle-audit-of-financed-cars-within-dealerships/
- Funded stock turns over continuously, so tags are re-issued per vehicle (consumable) and the presence feed only has value while the car is funded.

## Researcher observations
- Strong: the paid workaround (auditor visits, "one per 100 vehicles") and the failure mode (lender sues auditor for £1.8m) are both evidenced; buyers and lender names are concrete.
- Weak: no UK loss statistics; the incumbent UK direction is cheap number-plate-scanning apps with claimed 90% time savings, which a critic may treat as "good enough". Tamper-resistance of a tag on a car the dealer controls is the core technical risk.
- Better niche suggestion: the audit firms themselves (RGIS/SGS/ART-TKS) as buyers/partners — a presence feed lets them sell "continuous audit" without adding staff. Agricultural-machinery stocking finance (monthly audits, high unit values, rural sites) may be an easier first market than car dealers.
