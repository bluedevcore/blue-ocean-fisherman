## Idea (neutral statement)
- Problem: Debris can block council-owned culvert trash screens during storms and back water up into homes and roads. Councils inspect screens on rounds and after rainfall; telemetry exists at some sites but is reported to be costly, so many screens are unmonitored. The idea record cites Southend (~£15k clearance), Fife (~£3.4k install + ~£1.3k/yr quote) and Carmarthenshire (~£40k network); these figures are unverified here.
- Who suffers / who pays: Lead Local Flood Authority drainage teams and on-call crews; residents flooded downstream.
- Sensor concept: Upstream and downstream ultrasonic/radar level sensors (head-loss indicates blockage); LoRaWAN/NB-IoT; solar/battery; alerts on rising differential head with rainfall and an inspection/clearance log.
