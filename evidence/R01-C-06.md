## Idea (neutral statement)
- Problem: Under NRSWA s74, overrunning works on traffic-sensitive streets are charged up to £5k/day for the first 3 days then £10k/day; councils have levied ~£850k in months (Shropshire) and ~£483k in a year (Cambridgeshire 2024-25). Disputes hinge on when the site was really cleared, and contractors are charged when signs/barriers are left behind after the work is finished (Great Yarmouth reported huge delays from uncollected signs).
