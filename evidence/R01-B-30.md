## Idea (neutral statement)
- Problem: A deceased person's home can sit empty for months during probate. Unoccupied-home insurance typically carries conditions (periodic inspections; water off/drained or heating kept on in winter). If a pipe bursts and the conditions cannot be evidenced, the claim may be disputed.
- Who suffers / who pays: Beneficiaries and executors; private-client/probate solicitors acting as executors; unoccupied-property insurers.
- Sensor concept: Rented cellular kit: flow/pulse sensor on the incoming main, pipe/room temperature, door contact and PIR; alerts to a key holder and a weekly evidence pack for the insurer.
