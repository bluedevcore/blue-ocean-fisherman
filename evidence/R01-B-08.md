## Idea (neutral statement)
- Problem: Engineered (quartz) stone contains a high share of crystalline silica. Wet-cutting water suppression on bridge saws and hand tools may be switched off, blocked or frozen, and there is no record of whether water was flowing during each cut.
- Who suffers / who pays: Stone cutters/polishers (silicosis risk); small workshop owners (enforcement, claims). Payer would be the workshop owner/manager.
- Sensor concept: Inline flow switch/sensor on each tool's water feed plus a current clamp on the tool/saw; LoRaWAN or LTE-M; logs % of cutting time with water flowing, alerts on dry running, monthly COSHH control-measure report.
