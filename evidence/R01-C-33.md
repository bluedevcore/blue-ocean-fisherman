## Idea (neutral statement)
- Problem: Rural housing schemes off mains drainage use small package sewage treatment plants. If the air blower fails, the plant can turn septic and discharge poor-quality effluent to a watercourse, which may breach Environment Agency General Binding Rules (maintenance to manufacturer spec, record keeping).
- Who suffers / who pays: Rural housing association asset and compliance teams, neighbours, and local watercourses. The plant operator (landlord) pays for repairs, clean-up and any enforcement consequences.
- Sensor concept: Air-pressure sensor in the diffuser line plus blower current clamp plus final-chamber level sensor over NB-IoT/LTE-M; blower-fail and high-level alerts, plus service and desludge records for GBR compliance.
