# R01-D-18 — Little-used pool and spa changing-room showers — weekly Legionella flushing and monthly temperature checks done by hand

## Idea (neutral statement)
- Problem: Leisure centres and spas have dozens of showers and TMVs. ACoP L8 / HSG274 requires little-used outlets to be flushed weekly and sentinel temperatures to be checked monthly, so duty staff walk round flushing and water-hygiene contractors charge per outlet to take temperatures. Records are often incomplete, which is a recurring theme in HSE Legionella enforcement.
- Who suffers / who pays: Duty staff doing flushing rounds; the Legionella responsible person / buyer as proposed: Leisure Trust Compliance / Facilities Manager (Legionella responsible person)
- Sensor concept: Strap-on dual pipe thermistors on each shower/TMV supply detect use and record hot/cold temperatures, LoRaWAN or LTE-M gateway; auto-identifies outlets unused for 7 days (only those need flushing), logs sentinel temperatures continuously, exports an L8 compliance record.
