## Idea (neutral statement)
- Problem: Commercial property/theft policies for shops, trade counters and small warehouses can carry protection conditions or warranties (e.g. shutters closed, alarm set, keys removed). If a premises is left with a shutter partly up or the alarm unset, a theft claim may be declined or reduced.
- Who suffers / who pays: Branch/store managers and business owners; brokers handling declined claims.
- Sensor concept: Shutter-position sensor at the bottom rail plus alarm-set state from a panel output on a cellular node; alerts if the premises is not secured by a set time after closing, and a nightly compliance log.
