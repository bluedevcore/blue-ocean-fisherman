## Idea (neutral statement)
- Problem: The idea states that the Work at Height Regulations 2005 require scaffolds to be inspected at least every 7 days and after adverse weather. Scaffold contractors drive between sites to inspect and tag, and storms trigger bursts of inspections. Ties or bases that move between inspections can contribute to collapses. None of this has been verified.
- Who suffers / who pays: Scaffold contractors (inspection labour), site workers and the public, the principal contractor.
- Sensor concept: Clamp-on tilt and accelerometer sensors on key standards and tie points, plus a site wind-gust sensor, over LoRaWAN or LTE-M. It would alert on movement, provide local wind records to prioritise post-storm inspections, and keep a digital inspection register.
