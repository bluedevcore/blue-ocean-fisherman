# R01-D-19 — Hired site welfare units serviced on a fixed weekly round — waste tanks overflow or water runs out between visits

## Idea (neutral statement)
- Problem: Construction sites must provide toilets and washing water (CDM 2015 Schedule 2). Hire companies service welfare units weekly at ~£65–£120 a visit, so busy sites overflow or run dry mid-week (emergency call-outs, angry customers, welfare breaches) while quiet sites get visits they don't need.
- Who suffers / who pays: Site workers without toilets or water; site managers; hire service drivers / buyer as proposed: Hire company Welfare / Service Operations Manager
- Sensor concept: Ultrasonic level sensor on the waste tank, level/pressure on the fresh-water tank, weight on the gas bottle and generator run-hours, LTE-M; dynamic service routing (empty at ~80% full), pre-overflow alerts, proof of service and CDM welfare records for the contractor.
