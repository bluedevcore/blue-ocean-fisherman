## Idea (neutral statement)
- Problem: Blood samples ride for hours in courier vans; heat or cold exposure causes haemolysis and spurious potassium results, so samples are rejected or wrongly reported and patients recalled. ISO 15189 (UKAS) requires labs to control and monitor pre-examination transport conditions, but courier contracts give no temperature proof. (as stated in the idea record; unverified)
- Who suffers / who pays: Lab quality managers, GP practices recalling patients, couriers blamed without data; proposed buyer: Pathology Quality Manager / Pathology Logistics Manager (unverified)
- Sensor concept: Temperature logger + GNSS in each transport box, LTE-M, auto check-in on arrival at lab; per-route excursion report linked to rejected-sample rates
