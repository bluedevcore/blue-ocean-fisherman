## Idea (neutral statement)
- Problem: BS 5266-1 calls for monthly functional tests and an annual full-duration test of emergency lighting, with records, enforced under the Regulatory Reform (Fire Safety) Order 2005. Managing agents and housing associations with many small low-rise blocks reportedly pay contractors per visit to carry out and log these tests.
- Who suffers / who pays: Block managing agents and housing association compliance teams pay; contractors bear the travel time.
- Sensor concept: Retrofit relay on the existing test key-switch circuit plus stick-on photodiode tags on each emergency fitting, over LoRaWAN/LTE-M; remotely triggered monthly and annual tests with an automatic pass/fail log per fitting.
