## Idea (neutral statement)
- Problem: External dock and fire doors at food sites are left or propped open, a pest ingress route. Pest contractors and auditors flag it, but open duration is not measured.
- Who suffers / who pays: Food-site technical managers (the payer, possibly through the pest contractor); pest contractors.
- Sensor concept: A magnetic or accelerometer door-open sensor with a dwell timer and local buzzer, on LoRaWAN or LTE-M. It alerts after N minutes open and produces a weekly open-hours-per-door report correlated with pest activity.
