## Idea (neutral statement)
- Problem: Pellet silos and bunkers have no reliable level reading. When pellets run out, the boiler locks out, the building goes cold, and the site pays an emergency delivery premium plus a restart callout (~£500-£2k); care homes face welfare issues. Deliveries need ~3-7 days' notice.
- Who suffers / who pays: Site caretakers, residents and pupils, biomass maintenance contractors; buyer per idea record: Estates/Facilities Manager (schools, care homes, rural estates, holiday parks)
- Sensor concept: Dust-tolerant ToF/ultrasonic level sensor at the silo top plus boiler status contact, LTE-M; days-to-empty forecast pushed to the pellet supplier; low-level alerts.
