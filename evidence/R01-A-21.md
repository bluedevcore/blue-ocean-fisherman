## Idea (neutral statement)
- Problem: Oil storage regulations require bunding for commercial tanks over 200 L, but bunds fill with rainwater or oil unnoticed and single-skin tanks seep. Many policies exclude gradual pollution, so the clean-up (~£10k-£100k) and Environment Agency action fall on the business.
- Who suffers / who pays: Farm, school, church and small-business owners; facilities managers; buyer per idea record: Facilities Manager / Estate Manager (schools, care homes, rural businesses)
- Sensor concept: Battery bund sensor that distinguishes oil from water (conductivity/optical) plus tank level-drop analysis, NB-IoT/LTE-M; alerts on oil in the bund or unexplained level loss; annual compliance pack for insurer and EA.
