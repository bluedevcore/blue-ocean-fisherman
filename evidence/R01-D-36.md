## Idea (neutral statement)
- Problem: Farmers using professional rodenticides are expected to follow CRRU stewardship guidance (justification for permanent baiting, regular inspection). Farm-assurance schemes audit pest-control records, and records are said to be often incomplete.
- Who suffers / who pays: Assured livestock and arable farmers (the payer); wildlife, through secondary poisoning.
- Sensor concept: A bait-station activity sensor (IR or lid) with a bait-take estimate on LoRaWAN or LTE-M. It keeps an automatic inspection and activity log in CRRU/Red Tractor format and alerts when bait is taken.
