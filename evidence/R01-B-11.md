## Idea (neutral statement)
- Problem: Sprinkler installations designed to LPC Rules / BS EN 12845 are expected to have a weekly pump start test, fuel/oil checks and valve-pressure readings recorded. On lightly staffed distribution centres these checks can be skipped or recorded inaccurately, and pump-house frost, low diesel or flat starter batteries can go unnoticed until the system is needed.
- Who suffers / who pays: DC site/facilities teams, landlords and tenants (stock), and property insurers. Payer would be the site facilities manager or property manager.
- Sensor concept: Pressure transducers on installation/trunk main, vibration/current detection of jockey and main pump runs, diesel tank level, pump-house temperature and starter-battery voltage; NB-IoT/LTE-M; auto-built weekly test record, missed-test/frost/low-fuel alerts, shareable with sprinkler contractor and insurer.
