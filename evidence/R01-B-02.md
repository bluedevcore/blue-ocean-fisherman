## Idea (neutral statement)
- Problem: On cooling towers and evaporative condensers, the biocide or inhibitor day tank can run empty or the dosing pump can airlock. The system may then run undosed until the next contractor visit, raising microbial counts and Legionella risk. (Remediation costs and fine ranges in the idea record are unverified.)
- Who suffers / who pays: The duty holder (site engineering or facilities manager) pays for remediation and is exposed to enforcement. Water-treatment contractors carry service-level risk. Staff and the public carry the health risk.
- Sensor concept: Ultrasonic/ToF level (or load cell) on each chemical day tank, a vibration/current sensor proving the dosing pump strokes, and a sump temperature probe, NB-IoT/LTE-M, alerting contractor and site and auto-filling the weekly check record.
