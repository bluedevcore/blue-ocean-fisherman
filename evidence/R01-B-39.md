## Idea (neutral statement)
- Problem: Clean-agent suppression cylinders (HFC-227ea/FM-200, FK-5-1-12/Novec 1230, inert gases) can lose contents between service visits. A system below design quantity may not reach design concentration in a fire, and HFC systems fall under F-gas leak-check rules.
- Who suffers / who pays: Facilities/data-centre managers; fire-suppression service contractors.
- Sensor concept: Load cell under each cylinder or temperature-compensated pressure transducer on the gauge port; LoRaWAN/NB-IoT; continuous contents trend, leak alerts and a service-records export.
