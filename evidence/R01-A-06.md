## Idea (neutral statement)
- Problem: Depots with refuelling points and wash bays rely on oil separators that should be inspected about every 6 months and emptied. When oil or silt exceeds capacity, oil passes into drains, leading to an Environment Agency pollution incident, prosecution and clean-up (~£10k-£100k+). Meanwhile the paid tanker emptying is booked by calendar rather than need.
- Who suffers / who pays: Depot/yard managers; SHEQ managers; buyer per idea record: Depot Manager / SHEQ Manager of a haulage, bus or coach operator
- Sensor concept: Battery probe kit in the separator chamber: oil-layer probe (capacitive/conductivity) plus ultrasonic liquid/silt level; NB-IoT/LoRaWAN; alerts on oil depth and high level; empty-on-demand report for the contractor and an evidence log for the EA.
