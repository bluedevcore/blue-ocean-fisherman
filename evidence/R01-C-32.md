## Idea (neutral statement)
- Problem: Private (unadopted) sewage pumping stations serving housing estates can block or lose a float switch. Sewage may then back up into homes or gardens or overflow to watercourses. Many stations reportedly have only a local beacon alarm.
- Who suffers / who pays: Residents, housing association or estate-management asset teams, and the environment. The station owner or management company pays for clean-up and repairs.
- Sensor concept: Hydrostatic/ultrasonic wet-well level sensor plus pump current clamps over LTE-M with battery backup; high-level, pump-fail and run-hour alerts to the maintenance contractor.
