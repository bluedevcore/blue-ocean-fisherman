# R01-D-10 — Holiday-park lodge hot tubs tested by hand at every changeover — HSG282 logs patchy and Legionella risk

## Idea (neutral statement)
- Problem: Holiday parks and glamping sites with dozens to hundreds of lodge hot tubs must control Legionella under HSE's HSG282, which covers domestic-type hot tubs used in a business such as holiday-park rentals: frequent disinfectant/pH checks and records. Staff test by hand between guests and logs are patchy. Hot-tub-linked outbreaks lead to closures, enforcement and compensation claims; manual testing takes ~10–20 minutes per tub per day.
- Who suffers / who pays: Guests (illness), housekeeping and maintenance staff, park managers / buyer as proposed: Holiday Park Operations / Maintenance Manager (multi-lodge operators)
- Sensor concept: Floating or inline probe pod (ORP for disinfectant, pH, temperature) with replaceable probes, LTE-M; flags tubs out of range before guest arrival, auto-builds the HSG282 log per tub, alerts on heater/pump failure; service-contractor dashboard.
