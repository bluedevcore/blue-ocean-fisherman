## Idea (neutral statement)
- Problem: Audited food sites (BRCGS, retailer codes) are said to increasingly expect continuous electronic rodent monitoring. Large pest-control firms bundle proprietary systems; independent contractors may not have an equivalent, and routine visits still involve opening every station.
- Who suffers / who pays: Independent pest-control firms (the payer and reseller); food-site technical managers, who pay for the service.
- Sensor concept: A retrofit activity and lid/trigger sensor for standard bait stations and snap traps, on a LoRaWAN gateway with LTE-M backhaul. A white-label portal lets independents resell it, with audit-ready trend reports.
